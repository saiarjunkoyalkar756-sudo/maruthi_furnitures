Material Philosophy

Furniture that ages with grace.

Fast furniture breaks within years and ends in landfills. We design according to enduring architectural standards: solid CP teak timber that develops a rich natural patina, cold-rolled steel engineered with multi-tank powder coating, and doctor-approved rebonded orthopedic mattresses that nurture posture for a lifetime.

01

Seasoned Central Province Teak

Kiln-dried and naturally seasoned to prevent warping, seasonal swelling, and wood-borer intrusion.

02
