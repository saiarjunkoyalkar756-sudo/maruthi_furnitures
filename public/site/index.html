MATERIAL MANIFESTO

The Standard of Permanence

We construct pieces intended to outlive transient decor cycles. Every element is chosen for structural resistance and tactile nobility.

01

Grade-A CP Teak

Sustainably harvested Central Province teakwood, naturally dense with organic silica oils that repel termites and resist climatic humidity swings.

02

18-Gauge Steel

Industrial cold-rolled sheets passing through a rigorous 7-tank phosphating wash and electrostatic powder coating preventing oxidation for decades.

03

Spinal Ergonomics

Doctor-certified 7-zone multi-density rebonded foam cores engineered specifically to maintain horizontal vertebrae alignment throughout deep sleep.

04

Mortise & Tenon

Master joinery locking solid timber with traditional mortise-and-tenon craftsmanship. Zero squeaks, zero structural looseness over decades of use.
